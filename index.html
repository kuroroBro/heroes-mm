Hex Heroes

A hex-map fantasy strategy game. Capture mines. Grow your army. Fight it out on the hex battlefield.

Free · No ads · No accounts · Single-player vs. AI (v1)

How to Play

  1. Pick your faction — Human, Orc, Undead, Enkantos, Sunborn, or Yokai, each with its own 7-creature Castle roster — how many AI opponents (1-3) join the game, and a map size (x1/x2/x4 — bigger maps have proportionally more mines to fight over), then Start Game. Every hero (you and each AI) gets a different faction and its own Keep in a different corner of the hex map.
  2. Move — click a hex once to select it (shows its details), click that same hex again to confirm and move there (pathfound automatically). Each hex costs 1 of your day's movement points.
  3. Your Keep produces 500 gold every day on its own, on top of anything from mines — and you can permanently grow that number further by upgrading your Town Hall at the Castle (up to 3 levels, +1200 gold/day at max). Walking onto an unguarded mine or dwelling captures it instantly — mines add resources every day, dwellings unlock that creature type at your Castle (🏰 button), where it accrues daily and you spend resources to recruit it into your army. A dwelling can change hands again — if the enemy later takes it from you, you lose the ability to keep recruiting that creature (troops you'd already recruited stay yours either way). You can also build a dwelling directly from the Castle screen, for a resource cost, without ever finding or fighting for its map hex — a built unlock is permanent and isn't tied to holding any hex.
  4. Walking onto a guarded mine/dwelling, a monster stack, or the enemy hero starts a tactical hex battle.
  5. In battle, stacks act in Speed order. On your stack's turn, click a hex to move, or click an adjacent (or, for ranged creatures, any) enemy stack to attack. You can also Wait or Defend.
  6. Learn spells at your Castle, then cast them in battle (🔮 panel) by spending mana — casting is free and doesn't use up your stack's turn, but you can only cast once per round.
  7. Win a battle and surviving stacks return to the map with you. Lose your whole army and you respawn at your Keep with a fresh starting army and full mana — no permanent loss.
  8. Walking onto the enemy's Keep always starts a fight — their hero if they're home (with a home-turf defense bonus for them), or a militia drafted from their Castle if they're away. Win the raid and you loot 40% of their resources (their Castle is never captured). A siege battlefield has a wall with one open gate — standing wall hexes block movement (not ranged attacks or spells). As the attacker you can also Fire Catapult (free, once per round) at any standing wall hex to blast open a second gap.
  9. Click End Day when you're done moving — the AI takes its turn, then a new day begins.
  10. Defeat an enemy hero's army in direct combat the number of times set on the setup screen (default 3, tracked by the ⚔️ pill) to eliminate them. Every defeat before the last one just sends that hero home to respawn with a fresh starting army, same as losing to a neutral guard — no permanent loss until the final one. With more than one AI, eliminating a single rival doesn't end the game by itself — the game ends in combat once only one hero remains. Otherwise, the game ends at Day 30 and whoever has the higher Kingdom Score (mines, creature tiers, army) wins — unless you'd rather settle it with one decisive final battle against the current leader instead, which you'll be offered the choice to fight right then.
